My First Steps in Web Development

September 27, 2026 by Zoe James

This week I started building my own blog page using HTML and CSS. Working with semantic HTML helped me understand how headings, paragraphs, images and other elements fit together to create a complete webpage.

CSS made a big difference to the way the page looked. I experimented with spacing, colours and typography, then used Grid to organize the main content and sidebar. Learning how different layout tools work has made web development feel much more practical and interesting.

Finding Inspiration Outside

September 29, 2026 by Zoe James

Taking a break from a computer screen can be a good way to find new ideas. During a walk outside, I noticed how the landscape combines simple shapes, colours and open spaces. It reminded me that design ideas can come from ordinary things around us.

When I returned to my desk, I tried to bring some of that calm feeling into my webpage. I added more space between sections and kept the sidebar simple. I am learning that good design gives each element enough room to be clear and easy to read.
